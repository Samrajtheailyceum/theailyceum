About

A space for examining AI, not just reacting to it

The AI Lyceum is an independent platform founded in London by Samraj Matharu, a Certified AI Ethicist and AI & media strategist. The name references Aristotle's Lyceum — a place for walking and arguing, not broadcasting. That is the intended posture: curiosity first, positions second.

The public conversation about AI moves between hype and alarm, and skips the part in the middle where the actual decisions are made. The AI Lyceum exists for that middle ground — connecting technical builders, researchers, and policy makers to discuss responsible AI adoption and human values.

What lives here: a podcast with 36 published episodes, a weekly LinkedIn newsletter, The AI Republic (a Socratic dialogue on AI and philosophy), open-source projects including advertisingprotocols.com, and a community of 1,000+ members from research labs, agencies and universities.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is The AI Lyceum?

The AI Lyceum is an independent AI advisory, ethical assistance platform, podcast and community founded by Samraj Matharu in London, UK. It explores artificial intelligence through philosophy, ethics and governance, with 36 published conversations featuring guests from Google DeepMind, Cerebras, IBM Research and more. It operates year-round with a weekly newsletter, podcast and 1,000+ member community.

Who founded The AI Lyceum?

Samraj Matharu, an AI and media strategist and Certified AI Ethicist. He previously led global media operations and AI in media at Diageo and is a visiting lecturer at Durham University Business School. He also built advertisingprotocols.com.

What is the difference between The AI Lyceum and The Lyceum Project?

They are different organisations with no connection to each other. The AI Lyceum (theailyceum.com) is an independent, year-round platform founded by Samraj Matharu in London, with 36 podcast episodes, a weekly newsletter and 1,000+ community members. The Lyceum Project (thelyceumproject.ai) is a separate annual academic conference held in Athens, organised by Oxford's Institute for Ethics in AI and Stanford HAI.

Where can I listen to The AI Lyceum podcast?

The AI Lyceum podcast is free on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube and Amazon Music. All 36 episodes are available.
